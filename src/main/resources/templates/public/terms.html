Terms of use

Last updated 5 October 2026

Kosh is your employer’s payroll and HR system. By signing in you agree to use it as described here.

Your account

  • Accounts are created by HR or an administrator. Each account belongs to one person: do not share your password or let someone else work while you are signed in.
  • Sign out on shared computers, and tell your administrator straight away if you think someone else has used your account.
  • Your account is disabled when you leave the company, after your full and final settlement is paid.

Using Kosh

  • Open only the records your role is meant for. Kosh checks every request against your role, and every sign-in and change is recorded.
  • Keep salary, bank and tax details you see at work confidential, and do not copy them out of Kosh except for the purpose they were shared with you.
  • Upload only your own documents, as PDF, JPG or PNG files of up to 5 MB.

Calculations and filings

  • Kosh calculates pay and deductions from the salary structures, attendance and statutory rates set up by your company. Your company remains responsible for checking them.
  • Compliance files are marked “Prepared for filing - verify before upload”. Someone at your company must check each file before uploading it to the EPFO, ESIC, tax or bank portals and is responsible for filing on time.
  • The annual tax statement is labelled “Form 130-style statement generated by Kosh - not the official TRACES certificate”. The official certificate comes from TRACES through your employer.
  • A finalized payslip is never edited. If an administrator reopens a month, the old payslip is kept and marked void and a new version is issued; other corrections appear as arrears in a later month.

Availability and changes

Your employer decides when Kosh is available and may change these terms; the date at the top shows the latest version. Questions about these terms go to your HR team.
