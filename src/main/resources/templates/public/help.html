Help and questions

Answers to what people ask most about signing in, payslips, tax and approvals.

Signing in

How do I get an account?

HR or an administrator creates it and Kosh emails you a link to set your password. The link works once and expires after 48 hours; if it has expired, ask HR to send a new one. There is no self sign-up.

I forgot my password

Choose “Forgot password” on the sign-in page and enter your work email. The reset link works once and expires after 30 minutes.

Why is my account locked?

After five wrong passwords in a row, Kosh locks the account for 15 minutes to stop guessing. Wait, then try again or reset your password. If your account is disabled rather than locked, contact your administrator.

Payslips and pay

When is my payslip available?

As soon as Finance approves the month’s payroll. Kosh emails you, and the payslip appears under My payslips, where you can download it as a PDF at any time.

Why are my paid days fewer than the days in the month?

Paid days leave out loss-of-pay days: unpaid leave, absence without approved leave, and half of each half-day LOP. If you joined or left during the month, only the days you were employed count.

Attendance for the month closes on the company’s cut-off day, so raise a regularisation request before then if a day is marked wrongly.

Why is my bank account shown as XXXXXX4321?

Account numbers are masked on every screen and payslip so they cannot be read over someone’s shoulder. The full number is used only in the bank transfer file. To change your account, send a bank change request with a cancelled cheque or passbook page; HR approves it.

Tax and deductions

Which tax regime applies to me?

The new regime, unless you choose the old one. You can switch in self-service while the declaration window is open, and Kosh shows an estimate of your tax under both. When HR closes the window, your choice is fixed for the rest of the tax year.

How is my monthly TDS worked out?

Kosh projects your taxable salary for the whole tax year, works out the year’s tax under your regime, takes off the tax already deducted and spreads the rest over the remaining months. A bonus or arrears is taxed in the month it is paid.

Is the annual tax statement my Form 130?

No. Kosh’s statement is a working copy of the same figures, labelled “Form 130-style statement generated by Kosh - not the official TRACES certificate”. The official Form 130 comes from TRACES through your employer.

Why do PF, ESI and professional tax appear on my payslip?

They are deductions the law requires. PF goes to your EPFO account, matched by the company. ESI covers employees below the wage ceiling for medical care and is also matched by the company. Professional tax is a Maharashtra state tax set by salary slab. The company’s shares appear on your payslip under employer contributions.

Managers and approvals

What can my manager see?

Your leave, attendance and reimbursement requests, so they can approve them. Managers never see salary figures.

Who approves a payroll run?

Payroll calculates and submits it; someone in Finance approves or rejects it. The person who submitted a run can never approve it, even with both roles. Salary revisions and bank changes also need a second person.

Still need help?

For pay, tax or leave questions, contact your HR or payroll team. For access problems, contact your Kosh administrator. Include the month and your employee code so they can check quickly.
