Everything a payroll month needs

One system for HR, payroll, finance, managers and every employee, with each person seeing only what their role allows.

  • Employees and salary structures

    Job, statutory and bank details in one record, with PAN and account numbers encrypted. Salary revisions are effective-dated and approved by a second HR user.

  • Attendance and leave

    Leave accrues every month and managers approve requests for their own team. Closing the month works out paid days and loss-of-pay days for payroll.

  • Statutory deductions

    PF, ESI, professional tax and TDS under both tax regimes, calculated from effective-dated rate tables that an administrator updates when the law changes.

  • Maker-checker approval

    Payroll submits a run and Finance approves it. Nobody approves their own work, and every change of state is recorded with who made it and when.

  • Payslips and self-service

    Employees download payslips, choose a tax regime, declare investments and claim reimbursements from their phone, without emailing HR.

  • Compliance and bank files

    PF ECR, ESI contribution sheets, professional tax summaries, TDS statements and bank transfer files, prepared for filing and checked before upload.

How a payroll run works

Four steps, each one owned by a named person and recorded in the audit log.

  1. Close attendance and lock inputs

    HR closes the month’s attendance. Payroll adds overtime, bonuses and one-off deductions, then locks the inputs.

  2. Calculate and review

    Kosh calculates every payslip and flags what needs attention: missing bank details, missing PAN, large changes from last month.

  3. Approve

    Payroll submits the run and Finance approves or rejects it with a comment. Approval freezes the payslips exactly as calculated.

  4. Pay and file

    Payslips reach employees, the bank file goes to the bank, and the PF, ESI, PT and TDS files are ready before their due dates.

Statutory compliance

  • PF

    Provident Fund

    Employee and employer shares, EPS and EDLI, and the monthly ECR file.

  • ESI

    Employees’ State Insurance

    Coverage tracked by contribution period, both shares and the monthly return.

  • PT

    Professional tax

    Maharashtra slabs by gender, the February adjustment and the annual cap.

  • TDS

    Tax on salary

    New and old regime, monthly deduction and the annual statement for each employee.
