Privacy notice

Last updated 5 October 2026

Kosh holds personal data so that your employer can pay you correctly and meet its legal duties. This notice explains what is kept, who can see it and how it is protected.

Who is responsible

Your employer decides what Kosh is used for and is responsible for the data in it, as the data fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Questions about your own data go to your HR team.

What Kosh holds

  • Identity and contact details: name, date of birth, gender, addresses, mobile number, personal email and an emergency contact.
  • Employment details: employee code, department, designation, location, manager, joining date and exit details.
  • Pay details: salary structure and revisions, attendance, leave, payslips, loans and reimbursement claims.
  • Statutory details: PAN, UAN, ESI number, tax regime and investment declarations with their proofs.
  • Bank details: bank name, IFSC, account number and account holder name.
  • Account activity: sign-ins, failed sign-in attempts and changes you make, with the time and the network (IP) address they came from, kept in an audit log that only administrators can read.

Kosh never stores Aadhaar numbers.

Why it is used

To calculate and pay salaries, deduct and deposit PF, ESI, professional tax and income tax, produce payslips and statutory returns, run leave and attendance, and settle dues when someone leaves. It is not used for marketing and is not sold.

Who can see it

  • You can see your own profile, payslips, leave, attendance, tax declarations and claims.
  • Your manager sees your leave, attendance and reimbursement requests, never your salary.
  • HR, payroll, finance and administrators see what their role needs. Salary figures are limited to these roles.
  • Outside Kosh, data goes only where the law or your pay requires it: the bank that pays your salary and the EPFO, ESIC, professional tax and income-tax authorities.

How it is protected

  • PAN and bank account numbers are encrypted in the database. Account numbers are shown masked, as XXXXXX4321, everywhere except the bank transfer file.
  • Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes. Accounts lock for a while after repeated failed sign-ins.
  • Changes to salaries, bank details and roles are recorded in the audit log, and the most sensitive ones need a second person to approve them.
  • Uploaded documents are kept outside the public website and are only served to people allowed to see them.

How long it is kept

Payroll, tax and statutory records are kept for as long as labour and tax laws require employers to keep them, including after you leave. Other data is removed when it is no longer needed for those purposes.

Cookies

Kosh sets one session cookie when you sign in, and a remember-me cookie only if you ask for it. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Your choices

You can check your details in self-service and ask for corrections to your address, mobile number, emergency contact or bank account; HR reviews each request. For anything else, including a copy of your data, contact your HR team.
